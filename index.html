ESR 1.0 to ESR 2.5 Radar Upgrade & Emergency Harness Mod

Emergency Tail Harness Repin (When P/N 617-3216 is Out of Stock)

1. Move PIN 3 (Black 212 - Ground) to PIN 4 on the radar connector. 2. De-Pin PIN 1 (Yellow 166 - +12V Unswitched Battery) and heat-shrink/tape it back to the harness (ESR 2.5 only uses switched 12V on Pin 10). 3. Install blanking plugs into unused cavities. 4. Notch the radar mounting bracket (P/N 511-9464) per M0164397 to clear the outer case ribs on the ESR 2.5 radar.
